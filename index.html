2026 critical changes: 1) Visa funds proof now €877.50/mo since Aug 1, 2026 (was €615). 2) CAF housing aid (APL) since July 1, 2026: only EU/EEA/Swiss + non-EU with scholarship or job. Most non-EU without job/scholarship are no longer eligible.

1. Pre-arrival (3–6 mo before)

Campus France / Études en France, VLS-TS student visa (€99), housing proof for 3 months, funds proof, CVEC €105/yr, insurance.

2. Arrival week

Validate VLS-TS on ANEF within 3 months, French address, timbre fiscal ~€100, bank RIB, SIM €10–25, home insurance €80–100/yr, transport pass.

3. First 3 months

Sécu (Ameli), CAF if eligible, titre de séjour renewal (4→2 months before expiry), CROUS Resto U €3.30 / €1, jobs max 964h/yr (~20h/wk).

Visale — free public guarantor

  • Open to students 18–30, any nationality. Apply on visale.fr before signing lease.
  • Accepted by most CROUS, residences, private landlords.
  • Alternatives: bank guarantee, Garantme/SmartGarant (paid ~3.5% rent).

Scam checklist

  • Never pay before visiting (or video visit) + written lease.
  • Never wire via Western Union / crypto. RIB + lease in same name.
  • Landlord must give quittance de loyer; no “hotel/Airbnb” for CAF.
  • Ask for attestation de loyer for CAF file.
